# Web detectors on known texts

Generated by `web_controls.py analyze`. Do not edit by hand.

Each detector was run on published human scientific prose from before ChatGPT (some from the field of the document that prompted the run), on AI-written texts (Claude and GPT), on human texts rewritten by an AI, and on the human texts with their sentences shuffled (a check that the detector is not just recognising papers it was trained on). A text counts as flagged when the tool scores it 0.5 or more.

LR = likelihood ratio, with add-one smoothing so a small sample never gives infinity: how much more likely the tool's verdict is if the text is AI-written than if it is human. evidence.py counts a tool only with at least 5 human and 5 AI texts and an AUROC of 0.6 or more.

## Limits

- The samples are small: one more miss among 10 AI texts moves a detection rate by 10 points.
- The AI texts come from a few models prompted to write like scientists. Other models, careful prompting and human editing after generation are harder to catch.
- Sites change their models without notice. Re-run monthly (`watch.py`) and after any visible change.
