# Validation of the ai-text-detection thresholds

Generated by `calibrate.py` on 2026-11-15. Do not edit by hand.

Corpus: 1115 human windows from 798 documents written before ChatGPT (abstract, article, grant, review); 111 AI windows from 108 documents (generators: claude-haiku, claude-opus, claude-sonnet); 0 AI-polished human windows; 0 of the user's own labelled cases. Windows are ~300 words. Thresholds are set on half of the human documents; every rate below is measured on the other half (and on AI text never used to set them).

Read 'past 1%' as: share of windows the detector flags at its 1% false-positive operating point. For human text that share is the false-positive rate (should be near 1%); for AI text it is the detection rate.

## Limits

- The AI side is text from the generators listed above, prompted to write like a scientist. Other models, careful prompting, and human editing after generation lower detection rates; published evaluations (RAID, Dugan et al. 2024) show the same.
- The human side is published, edited text from 2015-2021. Unedited drafts, very short texts, and formulaic sections (methods, boilerplate) can behave differently.
- Every user-labelled case added with `ledger.py` and `--with-local` makes these numbers more representative of the documents the user actually receives.
