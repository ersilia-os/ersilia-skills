## The models

### Annotation — 2 models

- [Ctibor Škuta et al., *Nucleic Acids Research 2024*](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkae904) 🎯 — **Antimicrobial activity prediction from EU OpenScreen data** ([`eos3f8h`](https://github.com/ersilia-os/eos3f8h), Czech Academy of Sciences, Institute of Molecular Genetics). Predicts activity against seven reference pathogens — *A. baumannii*, *C. albicans*, *E. coli*, *E. faecalis*, *K. pneumoniae*, *P. aeruginosa* and *S. aureus* — from single-point inhibition screens of the ~100,000-compound EU OpenScreen library, taken from the European Chemical Biology Database. Škuta and colleagues produced that database; Ersilia trained these classifiers on it with LazyQSAR v3, reporting a mean AUROC of 0.94 across 5-fold cross-validation. · [code](https://github.com/ersilia-os/eu-openscreen-antimicrobial-tasks) · `ersilia fetch eu-openscreen-hts` · GPL-3.0-or-later

- [Narek Petrosyan et al., *Journal of Chemical Information and Modeling 2026*](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.5c02953) 🧪 — **HADES Oral Drug-Likeness** ([`eos3xhm`](https://github.com/ersilia-os/eos3xhm), Denovo Sciences Inc (Yerevan)). Scores how closely a compound resembles an approved oral drug, with 0.63 as the authors' recommended cut-off. Averages probabilities from five tree and boosting classifiers over 298 features combining Mordred descriptors, ADMET-AI predictions and QED terms, trained on 1,177 approved oral drugs against 5,307 non-drugs from ChEMBL, ZINC and GDB. The authors show scores rising across clinical phases and falling for orally toxic and chemically implausible structures. · [code](https://github.com/Narek-Petros-yan/HADES) · `ersilia fetch hades-oral-druglikeness`

### Representation — 4 models

- [Emily Nguyen et al., *2026*](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2606.11382) 🧩 — **GLACIER Molecular Embeddings** ([`eos5g6m`](https://github.com/ersilia-os/eos5g6m), University of Southern California). A multimodal student-teacher foundation model that turns a SMILES string into a 512-dimensional embedding. It fuses three views of a molecule — a message-passing graph encoder, a SMILES transformer and physicochemical descriptors — through a geometry-aware module, distilled from larger teacher models by contrastive learning. Intended as a general-purpose featurizer for downstream property prediction. · [code](https://github.com/eemokey/glacier) · `ersilia fetch glacier-embeddings`

- [Robin Winter et al., *ICML 2026*](https://openreview.net/forum?id=pB6WAdnRDR) 🧩 — **SAND Shape-Aware Descriptor** ([`eos5mnx`](https://github.com/ersilia-os/eos5mnx), Pfizer). Turns a molecule's 2D structure into a fixed-length embedding that captures its 3D shape without generating any conformers, so cosine similarity between vectors approximates 3D shape overlap. Useful for rapid retrieval of shape-similar molecules in ligand-based virtual screening. A GINE graph encoder producing 512 dimensions, trained at Pfizer and presented at ICML 2026. · [code](https://github.com/pfizer-opensource/SAND) · `ersilia fetch sand-shape-descriptor`

- [Yuheng Ding et al., *Nature Machine Intelligence 2026*](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42256-026-01226-8) 🧩 — **NaFM Natural Product Embeddings** ([`eos6pj2`](https://github.com/ersilia-os/eos6pj2), Peking University). A scaffold-aware graph foundation model for natural products, turning a SMILES string into a 1024-dimensional embedding. Pretrained on the COCONUT natural-product database with contrastive and masked-graph learning, it retains scaffold and side-chain information that general-purpose featurizers tend to lose. The authors apply it to taxonomy classification, genome mining and virtual screening. · [code](https://github.com/TomAIDD/NaFM-Official) · `ersilia fetch nafm-embeddings`

- [Sergey Sosnin, *Journal of Cheminformatics 2024*](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13321-024-00888-z) 🗺️ — **MolCompass Chemical Space Projection** ([`eos19dk`](https://github.com/ersilia-os/eos19dk), University of Vienna). Projects a SMILES string onto a 2D map of chemical space with a pretrained parametric t-SNE network, deterministically — structurally similar compounds land together every run. Built on 2048-bit ECFP fingerprints and trained on 1.56 million ChEMBL v23 structures. The authors intend it for exploring chemical space, checking a QSAR model's applicability domain, and spotting activity cliffs where similar compounds get inconsistent predictions. · [code](https://github.com/sergsb/molcomplib) · `ersilia fetch molcompass`

### Sampling — 4 models

- [Manuel Ruiz-Botella et al., *Nature Machine Intelligence 2026*](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42256-026-01229-5) 🎨 — **CoCoGraph Formula-Preserving Generation** ([`eos55vx`](https://github.com/ersilia-os/eos55vx), Universitat Rovira i Virgili). Generates 100 chemically valid molecules sharing the exact atomic composition of an input compound, via a constrained graph diffusion trained on 2.25 million molecules from PubChem, ChEMBL, ZINC and NIST. Validity is guaranteed by construction rather than learned: every diffusion step swaps bonds while preserving atom valences. In a blind test, 121 organic chemists distinguished generated from real molecules only 62% of the time. Restricted to molecules of 5–70 atoms including hydrogens. · [code](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18940151) · `ersilia fetch cocograph-formula`

- [Manuel Ruiz-Botella et al., *Nature Machine Intelligence 2026*](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42256-026-01229-5) 🎨 — **CoCoGraph Small-Fragment Inpainting** ([`eos6a1h`](https://github.com/ersilia-os/eos6a1h), Universitat Rovira i Virgili). The inpainting counterpart to CoCoGraph's formula-preserving model: it attaches a small fragment of 2–5 heavy atoms, drawn from a library of frequent substructures, then refines the result with the same constrained graph diffusion. Bond-pair swapping keeps every intermediate valence-valid, so chemical validity is guaranteed rather than filtered for afterwards. · [code](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18940151) · `ersilia fetch cocograph-small`

- [Seul Lee et al., *preprint 2025*](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2501.06158) 🎨 — **GenMol Scaffold Decoration** ([`eos84nf`](https://github.com/ersilia-os/eos84nf)). Given a scaffold with attachment points, decorates it with novel side fragments to produce new drug-like molecules, using masked discrete diffusion over the fragment-based SAFE representation. Trained on ZINC and UniChem. The authors report better validity, quality and diversity than the earlier autoregressive SAFE-GPT, and substantially faster sampling through non-autoregressive parallel decoding — aimed at hit-to-lead exploration around a known scaffold. · [code](https://github.com/NVIDIA-Digital-Bio/genmol) · `ersilia fetch genmol-scaffold-decoration`

- [Bruno N. Falcone et al., *Journal of Chemical Information and Modeling 2026*](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.6c00689) 🎨 — **PyMolGen Drug-Like Molecule Generation** ([`eos8zvb`](https://github.com/ersilia-os/eos8zvb), University of Nottingham). Generates analogues of a parent compound by deriving fragment-combination rules from ChEMBL: starting from a core structure, new molecules are sampled according to the database's fragment-bond frequencies, deduplicated and checked for RDKit validity. A deliberately simple, database-driven alternative to a learned generative model. · [code](https://github.com/HirstGroup/PyMolGen) · `ersilia fetch pymolgen`
